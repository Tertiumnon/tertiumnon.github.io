# Halloween YouTube Theme

Something is hiding in YouTube these days. Ghosts? Skeletons? Something else? A witch may steal the pumpkin from the logo, a spider may drop in from the top of the screen, and a swarm of bats may cross the page whenever you open a video. Click them if you dare, and remember: the moon watches us all.

Open YouTube. The theme adds a pumpkin progress scrubber, a pumpkin YouTube logo, a pumpkin in the corner, a spider that lowers on a thread from the top of the screen, a witch who takes the logo's pumpkin and carries it away, a skeleton, drifting ghosts and a night-sky background. Comment avatars are swapped for random Halloween faces: a pumpkin, a ghost, a skeleton, a witch, a zombie, a vampire, a red devil, a mummy or a black cat.
